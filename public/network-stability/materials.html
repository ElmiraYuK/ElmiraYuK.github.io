What the construction proves.
And what it does not.

1. The actual stochastic model

The physical graph is finite and fixed. Vertices fail at rate κ(1−q) and recover at rate κq. The working graph is the induced subgraph of available vertices; no retriangulation is performed after a failure. Sources receive Poisson arrival attempts. Failed sources reject new attempts, while accepted jobs wait during failures. On a working processor-sharing station the total service rate is μ; an infinite-server station gives each job rate μ.

At the first service completion a job either exits or receives its destination and makes its first routing step in that same event. Subsequent moves require service at the intervening station and a newly chosen shortest route. An unavailable or unreachable destination causes loss. Thus stability means positive Harris recurrence of the joint queue/environment process, not successful delivery of every job.

2. Finite profiles, not a stationary product-form assumption

For a working source, pb,k(q) is the probability that the first routing step goes to the terminal bottleneck. The excursion profile measures other non-terminal first steps. Put Φ(q)=Σk(γk/μ)(1−εk)pb,k(q) and e(q)=Σkpexc,k(q).

A strict background budget, sup[γmax/μ+e(q)/(1−q)]<1 on the relevant compact set, permits a corrected Lyapunov argument at all sufficiently large finite switching speeds. The terminal bottleneck then receives a genuine stationary input Jb, with the conditional bounds

Φ − Cμ/κ ≤ Jb/(qμ) ≤ Φ + e + Cμ/κ.

Only the direct part has an O(1/κ) approximation by Φ. The excursion term need not vanish as κ grows. Queue lengths and the environment are not assumed independent under the joint stationary law.

3. Why there is a Poisson equation

For the finite environment generator A and its semigroup Pt, the centred resolvent is Rf=∫₀∞(Ptf−πf)dt. It solves ARf=πf−f and πRf=0. This is a finite system of linear equations, not a spatial differential equation or a fluid limit. Independent refresh coupling gives ||Rf||∞≤Hm osc(f) for an observable of m spins.

For an integrable stationary joint process and an observable ψ of queue state, the same construction bounds |ν(ψf)| by ||Rf||∞ ν(vψ)/κ, where vψ is the absolute rate of variation caused by queue jumps. This controls the bias of environment observations at service events. Its applicability relies on autonomous environment dynamics and separate environment and queue jump channels.

4. Wheel realisations and hubs

The regular construction has 3K rim vertices and one central vertex. Its 6K edges give mean degree 12K/(3K+1)<4, but the hub degree is 3K. It is sparse in edge count and emphatically not hub-free. Even the general criterion assumes that the terminal bottleneck is adjacent to all K distinct destinations, so it does not by itself provide uniformly bounded degrees as K grows.

For Euclidean weights, the short arc, two spokes and long arc have lengths 4 sin(π/(3K)), 2 and 2(3K−2) sin(π/(3K)). Their strict order gives pb,k=q²(1−q) and pexc,k=q3K−2(1−q)². The construction works on open geometric neighbourhoods for every K≥9, not only at one symmetric picture.

5. A precise random-graph consequence

Fix K and take small disjoint discs of radius r around all 3K+1 template points. Choose r small enough to preserve the Delaunay and routing inequalities and to keep every triangular face circumdisc inside B(0,3). Let E be the event of one Poisson point in every small disc and no other point in the guard window.

P(E) = (λπr²)3K+1 exp(−9λπ) > 0.

There is no factorial: distinct discs label their points. The empty circumdiscs preserve every wheel edge after outside points are added. Conversely, a Delaunay edge of the full process between two selected vertices would also be an edge of the selected subset. The inherited finite graph is therefore exactly the certified wheel.

Disjoint guard windows give independent events and success probability 1−(1−p)M in M trials. Infinitely many successes almost surely establish occurrence, not typicality. The queueing result applies to each autonomous induced finite network. It does not establish stability of the full spatially interacting network or suppress external edges and traffic.

6. Current limits and future directions

The general criterion is sufficient, not a complete phase diagram. It uses a terminal bottleneck, an autonomous environment and a sufficiently fast regime. The switching threshold may depend on K. Several bottlenecks with feedback, hub-free bounded-degree realisations, moderate switching and interacting random spatial carriers remain separate research questions. No claimed result here depends on those questions being resolved.
